# Privacy Policy — ClipFlow Redes

*Last updated: November 26, 2026*

ClipFlow Redes (shown as "Clipflow Studio" on some developer platforms) is a free Windows desktop app that lets
you schedule and publish your own videos and review their performance. It is developed by SamHDQ.

## Where your data is stored
Everything is stored **only on your own computer**. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data.

## What the app accesses
Only after you sign in on the official page of each platform (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram), and only for
the permissions you accept:

- Basic profile information (name, avatar, account ID) and account statistics (followers, likes, video count).
- Your list of videos and their metrics.
- Comments on your content (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram), so you can read and reply to them.
- The ability to upload and publish the videos you choose.

The app never sees your password.

## How it is used
To publish what you schedule, to show your statistics and to let you answer comments. If you add competitor
accounts, the app stores their public figures on your computer. The built-in AI assistant runs locally.

## Sharing
Your data is not sold, rented or shared, and it is not used for advertising. The app talks directly to the official
platform services. It also connects to GitHub, Hugging Face and the tool publishers' sites to download updates,
tools and AI models; no data from your social accounts is sent to them.

## Security
Access tokens and app secrets are encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows user.

## Deleting your data
- Disconnect an account inside the app to delete its access tokens.
- To delete everything, uninstall the app and delete the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipFlowRedes`.
- You can also revoke access from each platform:
  TikTok (Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions),
  Google (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), Facebook and Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites).

## YouTube
The app uses YouTube API Services. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13.
